# Trademark pre-clearance

## What this is not

This is **preliminary screening to reduce risk before a decision**. It is
not legal clearance and never substitutes for a professional search and
opinion. Say this explicitly in every report.

Never write:

> "trademark available for registration"

Write:

> "no material collision found in preliminary research"

## Offices to search

| Scope | Office | Database |
|---|---|---|
| Brazil | INPI | <https://www.gov.br/inpi/pt-br/servicos/marcas> |
| International | WIPO | Global Brand Database |
| United States | USPTO | TESS / trademark search |
| European Union | EUIPO | eSearch plus |

A strong international collision in software or technology should influence
the decision even when nothing exists yet in the home market. Brands that
succeed expand, and the conflict arrives with them.

## What to search

For each candidate, do not stop at the exact term:

- exact term
- prefixes and suffixes
- the root / radical
- similar spellings
- phonetic equivalents

Phonetic similarity is what actually blocks registrations. A name that is
spelled differently but sounds identical to a registered mark in the same
class is a real conflict.

## Nice classification

The current edition has 45 classes. For a technology company, prioritise:

| Class | Covers |
|---|---|
| **9** | Software, downloadable software, digital products |
| **42** | Software development, engineering, technology services, SaaS |
| 35 | Commercial and digital business services, when applicable |

Expand to other classes as the actual filing requires.

## Risk classification

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `LOW` | No similar mark found in relevant classes |
| `LOW-MEDIUM` | Similar marks, distant classes, no confusion likely |
| `MEDIUM` | Similar mark in adjacent class, or phonetic neighbour in class 9/42 |
| `HIGH` | Identical or confusable mark registered in class 9 or 42 |

`HIGH` eliminates. `MEDIUM` requires professional review before the
decision is committed.

## Sequencing

Trademark screening comes **after** collision research, not before. There is
no point paying attention to registrations for a name already owned by a
well-known developer tool — the market conflict eliminates it regardless of
the register.

## Required disclosure

Every trademark section must end with:

> Preliminary search only. A public web search returning no result is not a
> negative certificate and does not substitute for a search in the official
> database or for professional legal assessment before filing.
